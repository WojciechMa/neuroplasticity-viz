Assignment 14* (computational)
Neuroplasticity is not purely synaptic. Neurons can also change their intrinsic excitability by
altering ion-channel conductances, including those of sodium and potassium channels. Such
changes can shift a neuron’s firing-rate-versus-current (f - I) curve in response to prolonged
changes in activity.

1. Extract Patch-seq current-clamp recordings from the Allen Cell Types Database
https://celltypes.brain-map.org/data. Select cells with both electrophysiology and
transcriptomic data, since these modalities are not available for every cell.

2. Use transcriptomic labels to classify the cells as excitatory or inhibitory, then construct
empirical (f - I) curves for each group.

3. In a simple neuron model, vary leak conductance and compare the resulting (f - I) curves
for representative excitatory and inhibitory cells. Treat this as a model-based
perturbation, not evidence that the recorded cells underwent activity-dependent
plasticity.
