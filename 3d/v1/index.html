Neuroplasticity course: Activity-dependent synaptic refinement in 3D

The same model as on the 2D page, drawn as neurons. Spontaneous waves sweep across the ganglion cells of the retina, at the back of the eye. One retinal ganglion cell sends its axon through the optic nerve; the axon branches and makes one synapse onto a neuron of target A and one onto a neuron of target B, each standing for its population.

Brightness shows the firing rate, and glowing pulses travelling along the axons are action potentials, drawn more often the higher the rate. The thickness of each axon branch and the size of its synaptic bouton show the synaptic weight. Choose a scenario, press Start retinal wave, and rotate the view. The firing-rate plots and the maths are on the 2D page.
